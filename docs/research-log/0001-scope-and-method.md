## Question
What is this project trying to do, and how will we keep recommendations honest?

## What we did
- Defined goals (README).
- Defined an evidence grading scheme (A–D) and a source → finding →
  recommendation traceability chain (`docs/method.md`).

## Why this structure
Map style debates tend to collapse into taste. A grading scheme lets us say
"this is a convention" (C) vs "this has been measured" (A/B) vs "this is a
hunch" (D), and lets later contributors overturn a recommendation by pointing
at the specific evidence it rests on.
