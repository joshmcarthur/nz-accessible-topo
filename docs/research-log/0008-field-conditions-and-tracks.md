# 0008 — Field conditions and track symbology

**Date:** 2026-11-01

## Question
How are topo maps actually used in New Zealand, and what conditions break
them?

## What we did
- Searched for evidence about map reading under red headtorch light. Found
  only practitioner reports (grade D) — but the mechanism is simple physics,
  so we modelled it in the audit tool (log 0003).
- Revisited McDonald (2020) for the user-community critique of tracks.

## What we found
- F-21 – F-25. Red-only encoding is fragile in the field; lightness and
  shape are robust. Tracks are where users most want change, but access
  status is a data problem.

## What changed
- `docs/findings/field-conditions.md`, `docs/findings/tracks-and-access.md`

## Confidence & gaps
- No controlled study found on topo-map reading under headtorch light. A
  cheap experiment: print test swatches and photograph under common
  headtorches' red and white modes. OQ-08.
