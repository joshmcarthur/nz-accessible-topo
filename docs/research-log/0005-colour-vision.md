# 0005 — Colour vision deficiency and ageing

**Date:** 2026-11-01

## Question
Who can't distinguish which colours, and how much does that matter for a
topographic map?

## What we did
- Read Jenny & Kelso (2007) — the standard cartographic reference on CVD and
  the source of the Color Oracle simulator.
- Searched for ageing effects on colour vision; found a large (n=865) study
  of older adults, a lifespan colour-constancy study (n=185), and a review of
  tritan-like ageing losses.
- Cross-referenced against the audit numbers from log 0004.

## What we found
- F-01 to F-04 in `findings/colour-vision.md`.
- The biggest surprise: the population that matters most for *pale fills*
  isn't only the 8% of men with red–green CVD, it's **older map users**, whose
  losses are mostly blue–yellow and worsen in dim light. Trampers, hunters and
  SAR volunteers skew older; field use is often dim.

## What changed
- Added `docs/findings/colour-vision.md`; sources added.

## Confidence & gaps

- We haven't found research specifically on CVD and *topographic* map tasks
