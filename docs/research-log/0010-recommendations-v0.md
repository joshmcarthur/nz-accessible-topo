# 0010 — Draft recommendations v0

**Date:** 2026-11-01

## Question
Given findings F-01 – F-25 and palette experiment v0, what should the style
actually do?

## What we did
- Wrote R-01 – R-16, each citing findings and its strongest evidence grade,
  with an explicit "met when" test.
- Collected unresolved issues into `open-questions.md` (OQ-01 – OQ-12).

## How the main recommendations were arrived at
- **R-03 (NNW shading)** is the best-evidenced change: replicated
  experiments, and it happens to be physically correct in NZ.
- **R-05 (no italics)** directly contradicts a cartographic convention; we
  chose the empirical evidence (B) over convention (C) because colour can
  carry the "water" signal instead.
- **R-06 (font)** was decided by measurement: the "most accessible" candidate
  (Atkinson Next) fails a hard requirement (Ō/ō), while the incumbent (Open
  Sans) passes and has the largest x-height.
- **R-04, R-10, R-11** come from palette v0, which showed which problems
  colour can fix (contours) and which need shape (fills, closed tracks).

## What changed
