# Findings: tracks and access

## F-24 — Track symbology is the most criticised part of Topo50
Topo50 tracks are all black, symbolised much as in 1977, with three main
types (vehicle, foot, poled route) and a closed-route sign, and no
indication of public vs private access. A 2020 petition asked LINZ and the
Walking Access Commission to redesign them; the Commission's online map has
had to overlay coloured lines on the black tracks as a stop-gap.
**Sources:** S-MCDONALD-2020 (D — advocacy document, but detailed and
well-referenced).

## F-25 — Access status is not in the topo data
Access (public/private, gazetted walkway, DOC track) comes from other
datasets (Walking Access Commission, DOC). Showing it is a **data**
question as much as a design one, and is out of scope for a pure re-style of
LINZ layers — but the symbology should leave room for it (e.g. a
reserved channel such as casing colour/pattern).
**Sources:** S-MCDONALD-2020.
